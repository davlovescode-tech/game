Character converter

Turns Mixamo .fbx characters into small .glb files the game can load. Everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

  1. On mixamo.com pick a character, press Download, choose FBX Binary (.fbx), T-pose, With Skin. No animation needed.
  2. Drop the downloaded .fbx files (or the .zip Mixamo gives you) below. Several at once is fine.
  3. Pick which one is Daviccino, then press Download zip and unzip it into your repo's assets/models/ folder.
Drop .fbx / .zip files hereor click to choose (include the texture images too if Mixamo gave them separately)

Mixamo characters are Adobe's: their terms let you use them in a game, but not hand out the raw files. This tool only produces the in-game .glb. Read Adobe's terms before making your repo public.
